# 📖 Guides

Community-written guides for perler bead artists at every skill level.

---

## Getting Started

**What you need:**
- Pegboard (standard: 29×29, large: 59×29, round available too)
- Beads: Perler, Hama, Artkal, or Nabbi all work on the same boards
- Ironing paper (comes with most bead sets — buy extra)
- Iron set to medium heat, **no steam**

**Basic workflow:**
1. Design your pattern (on paper or at [perlerbeads.io](https://perlerbeads.io))
2. Place beads on pegboard following your pattern
3. Cover with ironing paper
4. Iron in slow circular motions until beads fuse (15–30 seconds per side)
5. Let cool completely before removing from board

---

## Ironing & Fusing

- **Under-fused:** beads pop apart easily. Iron longer.
- **Over-fused:** holes close completely, colors blend together. Iron shorter.
- **Sweet spot:** holes are still slightly visible, beads hold together firmly.
- **Both sides:** flip and fuse the back for a stronger, more even finish.

## Advanced Techniques

- **3D builds:** fuse flat pieces and connect with beads acting as hinges
- **Large-scale:** connect multiple boards, iron sections separately, join with tape
- **Gradients:** place beads in a diagonal pattern, alternating two colors
